Point at a card or a line to light its flows and read it in the line at the bottom. The wheel and a pinch zoom the camera. A drag moves the map. A click or a tap on a card selects it and opens its details. A double-click on a card with a small map goes into that map. Tab and Shift+Tab move the keyboard focus through the cards and the lines of the current map. Enter selects the focused card or keeps the focused line lit. Enter on the selected card that opens a map goes into that map. Esc closes the details, or goes out one level. In a nested map, the Back button of the bar at the top and the small picture of the map above also go out one level. After a move out, the card of the nested map is selected. The arrow keys move the map. The keys + and - zoom. F fits the current map. The list after the canvas gives the maps, the cards and the flows for a screen reader. Tab does not go to the list. Each link in the list moves the camera to its item. In the Sequences view, the left and right arrow keys go to the previous step and to the next step. In the States view, Tab and the arrow keys move the keyboard focus through the states and the transitions. Enter keeps the reading line of the focused item, and Esc removes it. In the Focus view, Tab moves the keyboard focus through the cards and the lines. The up and down arrow keys go to the card above or below. The left and right arrow keys go to the adjacent column. Home goes to the card in the middle. Enter on a card puts that card in the middle. In the History view, Tab goes to the cells. The arrow keys move the keyboard focus through the names and the cells. Home goes to the name of the row and End to the last cell. Enter on a cell keeps its reading line. Enter on a name shows that card on the map. Ctrl K, Cmd K or the / key opens Find. The ? key opens the list of the keyboard keys.

Shift Enter

Esc
