What you are looking at. A card is one part of this system: something a reader would point at and name. Every card is code in the tree today, and the check refuses one whose modules or entry are not in the facts. A line is something moving from one part to another, labelled with what it carries. A layer shows only the lines that answer one question, so the same map can be looked at several ways. A journey is one trip through the system, shown one step at a time.

What the marks mean. A dashed card is an actor outside the code. A dot in a card's top corner marks a note, which the panel shows. A dashed line is an unreviewed flow. Its panel distinguishes an import, shared module, or named mechanism from a source reviewed claim. Those structural facts alone do not establish the flow's direction or artifact. A card drawn with a second card behind it holds a map of its own, and the panel opens that map in place over this page.

How to use it. Click a component to highlight it and everything it connects to. Escape clears the selection. The arrow keys switch layers, or step a journey while one is on. Double-click a region's name to fit that region to the screen. Text is drawn at 11px and never smaller: at Fit it is scaled down, and zoom brings it back.
