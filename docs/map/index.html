The wheel and a pinch zoom the camera. A drag moves the map. A double-click on a card goes into the card. Tab and Shift+Tab move the keyboard focus through the cards of the current map. Enter on a card that opens a map goes into that map. Enter on a different card selects it. Esc goes out one level. On a touch screen, a tap on a card selects the card. A second tap on a card that opens a map goes into that map. The arrow keys move the map. The keys + and - zoom. F fits the current map. The list after the canvas gives the maps, the cards and the flows. Each link in the list moves the camera to its item.
